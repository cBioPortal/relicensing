# cBioPortal relicensing — Tier 1 Team Lead Consent Terms

**Document purpose:** Reference document describing the scope and terms for Tier 1 team lead consent  
**License transition:** AGPL-3.0 \-\> Apache-2.0  
**Consent collection:** https://github.com/cBioPortal/cbioportal/issues/12282

---

## Background

cBioPortal is proposing to relicense the in-scope repositories from AGPL-3.0 to Apache-2.0 under cBioPortal RFC 86\. This document describes the scope and terms for team lead consent. Team leads post their consent directly on the [Tier 1 consent issue](https://github.com/cBioPortal/cbioportal/issues/12282).

| Repository | URL |

## Consent statement

By posting consent on the [Tier 1 consent issue](https://github.com/cBioPortal/cbioportal/issues/12282), I confirm that I am the team lead (or an authorized representative acting on the lead's behalf) for the cBioPortal team listed, and in that capacity I consent to relicensing contributions made by the members of my team in the repositories above from AGPL-3.0 to Apache-2.0.

This Tier 1 team lead consent is not intended to replace individual Tier 2 outreach for contributors whose work may fall outside the scope of this team's activities.
