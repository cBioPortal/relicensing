# cBioPortal relicensing Team Lead Consent Sheet

**Document purpose:** Tier 1 consent document for cBioPortal core team leads  
**License transition:** AGPL-3.0 \-\> Apache-2.0

---

## Background

cBioPortal is proposing to relicense the in-scope repositories from AGPL-3.0 to Apache-2.0 under cBioPortal RFC 86\. This document captures consent from the leads of each core cBioPortal development team in one place.

## In-scope repositories

| Repository | URL |
| :---- | :---- |
| cbioportal (backend) | [https://github.com/cBioPortal/cbioportal](https://github.com/cBioPortal/cbioportal) |
| cbioportal-frontend | [https://github.com/cBioPortal/cbioportal-frontend](https://github.com/cBioPortal/cbioportal-frontend) |
| cbioportal-docker-compose | [https://github.com/cBioPortal/cbioportal-docker-compose](https://github.com/cBioPortal/cbioportal-docker-compose) |
| cbioportal-core (data importer) | [https://github.com/cBioPortal/cbioportal-core](https://github.com/cBioPortal/cbioportal-core) |
| session-service | [https://github.com/cBioPortal/session-service](https://github.com/cBioPortal/session-service) |

## Consent statement (applies to each approver below)

By approving below, I confirm that I am the team lead (or an authorized representative acting on the lead's behalf) for the cBioPortal team listed, and in that capacity I consent to relicensing contributions made by the members of my team in the repositories above from AGPL-3.0 to Apache-2.0.

This Tier 1 team lead consent is not intended to replace individual Tier 2 outreach for contributors whose work may fall outside the scope of this team's activities.
