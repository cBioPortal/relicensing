# Individual Contributor Outreach — Template

*Send by email if an address is available in git history or Slack; otherwise @mention the contributor on [relicensing#24](https://github.com/cBioPortal/relicensing/issues/24). The reasons, process and FAQ live on that issue, so the message stays short.*

---

**Subject:** cBioPortal is now Apache-2.0: please confirm your past contributions

---

Hi [NAME],

You contributed to cBioPortal (@[GITHUB_HANDLE], [N] commits). cBioPortal has moved from AGPL-3.0 to the Apache License 2.0 (RFC 86), and we're asking contributors we haven't heard from yet to confirm.

Could you reply to this email, or comment on https://github.com/cBioPortal/relicensing/issues/24, with:

> I, [your name], consent to the relicensing of my contributions to the cBioPortal repositories from AGPL-3.0 to Apache-2.0 as described in RFC 86.

The reasons, the process, and what happens if you'd rather not consent are all on that page. Questions are welcome at gaoj@mskcc.org.

Thank you for contributing to cBioPortal.

Jianjiong Gao, on behalf of the cBioPortal core team

---

*Sender notes: check the [status board](https://github.com/cBioPortal/relicensing/blob/main/docs/relicensing/contributor-consent-status.md) first; contributors covered by a team-lead consent need no message. Do not include other contributors' emails or the private matrix in any message.*
